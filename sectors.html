Who we serve

Public, infrastructure and private-development ecosystems

We work across the organisations that plan, build and operate Indian cities, and with the technology partners that help deliver.

Urban local bodies

Municipal corporations and councils that need planning, infrastructure, urban operations, road safety and decision support, in one evidence base.

What you need from us

  • Development plans, local area plans and mobility plans
  • Traffic operations: signals, corridors, parking and enforcement
  • Road-safety programmes with measurable outcomes
  • A city digital twin your departments actually use

Questions we answer

Which junctions do we fix first with the budget we have?
Can our existing CCTV network be turned into traffic and safety intelligence?

Relevant work: arterial corridor safety programme in Delhi; CSR-funded vision analytics for a municipal corporation and traffic police; district road-safety concepts.

Development authorities

Metropolitan and regional authorities responsible for master and development plans, growth management, infrastructure phasing and monitoring.

What you need from us

  • Regional and master plans with scenario evidence
  • Growth allocation and peri-urban management
  • Infrastructure capacity and phasing models
  • Plan monitoring dashboards and digital plan layers

Questions we answer

Where should the next million residents go, and what infrastructure must precede them?
What does each land-use scenario do to travel demand, emissions and water?

Relevant work: statutory planning and infrastructure capacity work at IIT Bombay's urban science centre; state drone mission for planning and governance.

Transport and road agencies

Highway authorities, state transport departments, traffic police and ITS operators: road safety, autonomous pilots, sensing, corridor analysis and asset intelligence.

What you need from us

  • Road safety audits and black-spot prediction
  • Adaptive signal and corridor programmes
  • V2X living labs and connected-vehicle pilots
  • National and state ITS architecture

Questions we answer

How do we run a coordinated corridor on the controllers we already own?
Which road-safety technologies are implemented and ready, and which are slideware?

Relevant work: Jaipur living-lab corridor; national road-safety technology conclave; national ITS architecture concept.

Developers and townships

Real-estate developers, township operators and campus owners: master planning, 3D analysis, mobility, infrastructure phasing and urban design.

What you need from us

  • Township master plans and urban design frameworks
  • Traffic impact studies and construction logistics
  • 3D visualisation and marketing-grade digital twins
  • Automated valet parking and smart-campus mobility

Questions we answer

What will parking and traffic look like at full occupancy, by phase?
Can construction traffic coexist with a live neighbourhood without stopping the programme?

Relevant work: construction logistics and traffic study for a major urban redevelopment in Mumbai.

Industrial and logistics campuses

Warehouses, ports, plants and logistics parks: automation, autonomous operations, IoT and digital-twin applications.

What you need from us

  • Autonomous forklifts and brownfield retrofits
  • Fleet telematics and driver-fatigue monitoring
  • Yard and warehouse digital twins
  • Mobility intelligence for logistics networks

Questions we answer

What is the optimal level of warehouse automation for our site, with numbers?
Can we retrofit the forklifts we own instead of buying autonomous ones?

Relevant work: connected-fleet telematics, driver-fatigue monitoring and autonomous-forklift programmes with a global logistics company; mobility intelligence engine for a logistics software firm.

Technology and engineering partners

OEMs, Tier-1 suppliers, ITS vendors, surveillance and sensor companies, and engineering consultancies, for joint delivery where hardware, software, civil or transport engineering is required.

What you need from us

  • Scenario generation and validation for ADAS and AD
  • India-tuned perception and video analytics
  • Tyre, road and vehicle interaction test campaigns
  • Automotive cybersecurity engineering and training

Questions we answer

How will our product behave on Indian roads, and can we prove it before launch?
Can an academic-grade method be productised on our hardware?

Relevant work: scenario engine for an automotive OEM; India tyre test campaign for a global tyre maker; video analytics with a surveillance major; cybersecurity programme for a two-wheeler OEM.
