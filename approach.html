Approach

Understand spatially. Test virtually. Plan holistically. Automate safely.

The city should be approached as an integrated system: understood spatially, evaluated through simulation, planned holistically, and supported by safe and intelligent automation. This is how we run every engagement, whatever its size.

The decision chain

Five stages, delivered whole or as modules

STAGE 01 / 05
Stage 01 · Field intelligence

Observe the physical city.

Connect urban reality to reliable spatial evidence through multi-scale sensing, on-ground data and contextual observation.

  • Drone, satellite and street-level imagery
  • Vehicle, roadside and wearable sensors; LiDAR, radar and vision
  • Telecom, app and crowdsourced mobility data
  • Surveys, soundscape and perception capture
OutputsValidated data foundationGap mapLive feeds
Urbasense · Connected system architecture
01 · OBSERVE

Collect the evidence

Imagery, vehicle data, surveys, sensors and perception studies. Outputs: a validated data foundation and gap map.

02 · MODEL

Build the twin

3D GIS, networks and asset registers. Outputs: a city, corridor or campus digital model your teams can query.

03 · SIMULATE

Test the options

Land use, mobility, infrastructure and safety scenarios. Outputs: quantified comparison of alternatives.

04 · PLAN

Decide and specify

Plans, corridor designs, interventions and phasing. Outputs: documents ready for approval and procurement.

05 · AUTOMATE

Deploy and monitor

Adaptive control, connected infrastructure, autonomy. Outputs: live systems with KPIs fed back to the model.

Business model

Projects create entry; software and IP create scale

A blended model is intentional. Early urban deep-tech adoption usually begins as a project or pilot before becoming a repeatable platform or licence. Our initial emphasis is planning and design assignments; recurring products are developed where repeated market need is demonstrated.

Planning and design assignments

Project fees for master, development and local area plans, mobility plans, infrastructure strategies and urban design.

Engineering proofs of concept and pilots

Paid or co-funded pilots for road safety, IoT, autonomous or robotic systems, with field validation on your corridor or site.

3D GIS and digital-twin implementation

Setup, integration, data engineering and configuration, then handover to your teams.

Software and analytics subscription

Recurring access to dashboards, simulation, monitoring and decision-support services.

Technology and IP licensing

Licensing of validated algorithms, modules and specialised workflows, including patented sensing and testing platforms.

Advisory and capacity building

Technical architecture for national and state programmes, independent evaluation, and training for agency staff and OEM engineers.

Principle one
India-first engineering: mixed traffic, two-wheelers, hawkers, monsoon, dust and heat are the design case, not the edge case. What works here exports.
Principles

What we hold ourselves to

  • Evidence before intervention. Every recommendation carries the data, the model and the before/after measurement plan behind it.
  • Open standards. OpenDRIVE, OpenSCENARIO, OpenCRG, FMI, CityGML and open APIs, so what we build is not locked to us or to one vendor.
  • Technical architect, not just vendor. We design programmes agencies can procure and operate themselves, the way IIT Bombay has for national road-safety initiatives.
  • Academic rigour at commercial pace. Peer-reviewed methods, patented platforms and a company that ships on a client's timeline.
  • Public value. Safety outcomes are reported openly; data governance, privacy and IP are settled in writing before work starts.
