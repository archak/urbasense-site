Platforms & IP

Built and tested in-house

A full-size autonomous vehicle, a scaled connected fleet, a scenario engine, living-lab corridors and a growing patent portfolio. The platforms we use on client work, available to partners under licence.

Licensing and partnership enquiries
Vehicles and testbeds

Simulate. Test at small scale. Deploy at full scale.

Full-size autonomous shuttle

A campus shuttle with drive-by-wire control, a LiDAR map of the IIT Bombay campus, real-time on-board localisation and autonomous runs on campus roads, entered in international competition and inaugurated by the Union Minister for Road Transport and Highways.

  • Modular perception: radar, LiDAR and vision fusion
  • Source-owned navigation and perception stack
  • Phase II: covered vehicle facility, instrumented corridor, proving ground

Scaled connected fleet

Autonomous cars running V2V and V2I on a scaled track mirrored in simulation, for repeatable, low-risk trials of signal plans, corridor strategies, parking systems and warehouse robotics, with automotive and technology partners.

  • Cyber-physical testbed with live digital twin
  • Hydraulic scaled forklift for warehouse automation trials
  • Patent-pending low-resource modular platform

Scenario generation engine

Ontology, reinforcement learning and generative AI generate safety-critical driving scenarios across scenario families, with a no-code template library, fast-preview search, natural-language and GUI input, OpenSCENARIO export and replay, and run-history and coverage metrics.

  • Docker and on-premise deployment
  • Coverage-driven test-suite generation
  • Indian traffic actors and behaviours

Living-lab corridors

A national-highway arterial in Jaipur with adaptive signals, committed SPaT access and V2X units; an enforcement and safety corridor in Delhi under a national road-safety committee; and the IIT Bombay campus as an instrumented network for autonomy.

  • Field validation for every algorithm we ship
  • Partner access for OEM and Tier-1 trials
  • Data pipelines into the city digital twin

Mobility intelligence engine

A domain language model and analytics engine for logistics and mobility that answers operational questions over fleet, shipment and network data.

  • Retrieval over operator data
  • Scenario and what-if queries
  • Airport autonomous-operations extension under discussion

Living travel demand model

An always-on, activity-based model of how a city moves, fed by telecom, app and crowdsourced data instead of a once-a-decade household survey, with a practice manual for national roll-out.

  • Daily-refresh origin–destination matrices
  • Scenario testing for plans and operations
  • Privacy-preserving data pipelines
