Why Urbasense

Cities are still planned, modelled and operated in silos. We close the loop.

Plans stop at static documents while real-time operations evolve separately. GIS, imagery, surveys and sensors create data, but the decision chain is fragmented. Autonomous systems need road, land-use and safety context they rarely get. We build the common spatial and operational layer that connects plans, models, assets, vehicles, sensors and decisions.

Granted and pending patentson transport sensing, testing platforms and emergency priority, filed through IIT Bombay
Living-lab corridorsin Jaipur, Delhi and on the IIT Bombay campus, where every algorithm is validated in real traffic
Full-size and scaled autonomya drive-by-wire autonomous shuttle and a connected scaled fleet mirrored in simulation
National programmestechnical roles with road-safety, ITS and drone missions at the national and state level

One urban system

One urban system. Five connected capabilities.

Urbasense sits at the centre of a connected pipeline: field data feeds spatial models, models feed simulation, simulation informs plans, plans drive automated systems, and the results flow back. Delivered end-to-end or as individual modules.

Stage 01 · Field intelligence

Observe the physical city.

Connect urban reality to reliable spatial evidence through multi-scale sensing, on-ground data and contextual observation.

  • Drone, satellite and street-level imagery
  • Vehicle, roadside and wearable sensors; LiDAR, radar and vision
  • Telecom, app and crowdsourced mobility data
  • Surveys, soundscape and perception capture

Problems we are solving right now

Automotive · Validation

Safety-critical scenario generation for automated driving

An ontology, reinforcement learning and generative AI produce standards-compliant test cases for ADAS and automated-driving validation, tuned to Indian traffic.

Public agency · Road safety

Arterial corridor safety and enforcement programme, Delhi

An intelligent enforcement network, corridor redesign and a one-year operating programme on one of the capital's busiest arterials, with a coordination committee in place.

Urban renewal · Mumbai

Construction logistics in one of the densest districts on earth

A coupled model of the road network, site logistics and construction schedule tells a redevelopment programme how many trucks the streets can absorb each day.

All projects
Built and tested in-house

Our own vehicles, testbeds and living labs

A full-size autonomous shuttle with drive-by-wire control and real-time LiDAR localisation runs on IIT Bombay campus roads. A scaled connected fleet mirrors it in simulation for repeatable, low-risk trials. Instrumented corridors in Jaipur and Delhi take the same algorithms to real traffic.
