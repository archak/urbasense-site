Projects

Engagements across planning, road safety, connected corridors, autonomy and logistics, led by our founders at IIT Bombay and carried forward through Urbasense. Projects are described by theme and sector; client names are listed on the Partners page.

Automotive · ADAS and AD validation

Safety-critical scenario generation for automated driving

A framework that generates safety-critical driving scenarios from an ontology, reinforcement-learning policies and generative AI: a no-code template library, a fast-preview search engine, natural-language and GUI input, OpenSCENARIO export and replay, and run-history and coverage tracking across scenario families. Built for a global OEM's India research centre and its teams in Europe.

LiveMobilityIndustry
Public agency · Road safety · Delhi

Arterial corridor safety and enforcement programme

Technical lead for a national road-safety committee's pilot on one of Delhi's busiest arterials: an intelligent enforcement network able to detect a wide range of violations, corridor safety redesign and a one-year operating programme, with a corridor coordination committee constituted and CSR funding being structured with an automotive partner.

LiveMobilityPublic sector
Urban renewal · Mumbai

Construction logistics and traffic modelling for a dense redevelopment

Independent study of how narrow roads, hawkers, two-sided on-street parking and travel-time restrictions constrain construction logistics and timelines for residential towers beside a suburban railway station. A four-layer coupled model: macro network, microsimulation, discrete-event site logistics and 4D schedule coupling, calibrated with truck GPS logs.

LivePlanningDevelopers
National research grant · Jaipur

Living-lab corridor: joint signal and vehicle trajectory optimisation

A multi-year national early-career research grant on a national-highway arterial in Jaipur. Signal timing and vehicle trajectories are co-optimised as coupled decision variables on an adaptive signal corridor with full API and committed-SPaT access, roadside and on-board V2X units, and industry partners across signals, V2X and vehicles.

LiveMobilityPublic sectorResearch
Global tyre manufacturer · India entry

India tyre-life test campaign and federated virtual proving ground

A tightly designed first India test campaign with the tyre mission (vehicle, tyre, load, route, climate, road events, maintenance) as the unit of analysis, backed by a physics-informed road model built from crowdsourced and fleet-seeded sensing on open standards (FMI, OpenCRG, OpenDRIVE), with tyre models as sealed FMUs.

CSR programme · Mumbai and a Tier-II city

Kerbside impedance and curve-speed video analytics

India-tuned vision analytics that convert kerbside violations into a quantified corridor impedance and an annual rupee-per-kilometre cost a city can use to justify enforcement, led by a curve-capable speed measurement engine that works where straight-road homography breaks down. Delivered with a surveillance-technology major for a municipal and traffic-police beneficiary.

In developmentMobilityIndustryPublic sector
Logistics software · Product programme

Mobility intelligence engine for logistics

A domain language model and analytics engine for logistics and mobility that answers operational questions over fleet, shipment and network data, taken from product vision through engineering realisation, plan and resourcing with a logistics software company. Follow-on work under discussion includes autonomous vehicles for airport operations.

Road-safety foundation · Nagpur Rural

Independent evaluation of a zero-fatality district programme

Fifteen months of realist and process evaluation of a district-level road-safety programme, using the context–mechanism–outcome framework, the Framework Method and COREQ reporting. Qualitative instruments covering senior officials through crash survivors, with research-council-compliant ethics and field agencies procured for data collection.

National conclave · IIT Bombay

National Road Safety Technology Conclave

Convened at IIT Bombay Research Park with a national road-safety committee and an ITS industry forum, funded by the automotive industry body: technologies selected by a jury from an open call, structured sessions and a half-day where the committee questioned presenting companies, for transport commissioners and traffic-police leadership from states and union territories across India.

CompletedMobilityPublic sectorEvents
Defence research · Off-road autonomy

AI-enabled situation awareness for mobility

A modular perception stack fusing radar, LiDAR and vision, with radar as the channel that keeps working in dust, fog and mist, extended to generate the outputs a navigation stack needs, starting on a cross-country four-wheeler. Weekly technical reviews, source-code delivery and an autonomous demonstration on the partner's site.

In-house platform · IIT Bombay

Full-size autonomous shuttle and scaled connected fleet

A full-size autonomous campus shuttle with drive-by-wire control, a LiDAR map of the IIT Bombay campus and real-time on-board localisation, entered in international student autonomous-racing competition and inaugurated by the Union Minister for Road Transport and Highways. Phase II, backed by alumni support, adds a covered vehicle facility, instrumented campus corridor and proving ground.

Mass-gathering authorities · Nashik and Ujjain

Crowd and traffic sensing for Simhastha Kumbh

4D imaging radar plus AI cameras for crowd and vehicle sensing that work at night and in dust, a crowd–vehicle intelligence platform, and a collaboration framework spanning crowd and mobility management, emergency response, operational simulation and digital twins, environmental monitoring and independent evaluation of deployed technologies.

In developmentMobilityPublic sector
Global logistics company · R&D programme

Connected fleets, driver fatigue and autonomous forklifts

Three logistics programmes: connected commercial-vehicle fleet telematics with a regulatory hook and telematics partners; physiological driver-fatigue monitoring; and an autonomous forklift for brownfield warehouses whose simulation phase closed the design (algorithms, sensor selection and placement, achievable precision) ahead of a scaled hardware build and full-size retrofit. Plus trusted electronic bills of lading for multimodal logistics.

In developmentMobilityLogisticsIndustry
National policy · Think-tank and ministry

National ITS architecture and the India Mobility Stack

A national intelligent-transport-systems architecture framed as digital public infrastructure in the lineage of Aadhaar and UPI: a layered stack, vertical rails, a multi-view architecture, service domains and a three-horizon roadmap, with a national road-safety observatory as the country's safety operating system. Presented as a national mission on intelligent mobility with IIT Bombay as technical secretariat.

ConceptMobilityPublic sectorPolicy
Telecom ministry · National capability

Mobility analytics from telecom and crowdsourced data

A national mobility planning capability built on mobile-network data, 5G/6G network intelligence and crowdsourced sources: a research report and practice manual, and proofs of concept being scoped with a public operator on data privacy, standards, business models and applications. Feeds the living travel demand model: an always-on, activity-based model of how a city moves.

Walkability challenges in Global South cities

Comparative research on what stops people walking in Global South cities, led from IIT Bombay's urban science group: audit methods, perception measurement and the policy levers that change pedestrian safety and mobility behaviour. High-resolution carbon emissions data for Indian cities

City-scale, high-resolution emissions accounting across more than a hundred Indian cities, giving planners and climate officers a common baseline for action plans and for testing what land-use and transport scenarios do to emissions.

State emergency medical service

Emergency response analysis and ambulance location-allocation

Spatial and operational analysis of a state's emergency ambulance service: demand, response times, coverage and location-allocation of ambulances and bases, a template for the emergency-response planning we now offer for cities and mass events.

National science agency · International academy

Healthcare informatics and everyday urban infrastructure

Information systems for healthcare access and the built environment, and an international academy-funded study of metering, services and everyday infrastructure in Indian cities. Together with a state climate action plan, these shape our urban health, energy and climate practice.

CompletedPlanningHealthResearch
Urbasense research

Soundscapes and sensory perception of urban environments

Doctoral research at IIT Bombay on how urban soundscapes shape individual experience, treating sound as a design quality rather than only as noise, with a review of tools and techniques for audio-visual assessment of urbanscape. Now the basis of our perception-study method for streets, markets and public spaces.

In developmentPlanningResearch
Wearable sensing programme

Driver and rider fatigue from wearable sensing

Research-grade wearable glasses as the reference sensor for early fatigue detection from gaze, pupil and physiology, then transfer to low-cost in-cab channels for the Indian market: a dashboard camera, a steering-wheel touch sensor and seat-belt heart rate. Thrusts cover two-wheeler riders, car and truck drivers, and walkability audits.

Automotive cybersecurity engineering programme

A multi-day programme at IIT Bombay for an OEM's R&D engineers, anchored on ISO/SAE 21434 with AIS-189 (cybersecurity management systems) and AIS-190 (software update management systems): practical, intermediate-level engineering of cybersecurity into vehicles.

Automated valet parking and smart-campus mobility

Cars drive themselves to a free slot and return on request, guided by infrastructure cameras and sensors, with slot detection, parking guidance and EV-charging bay allocation. Proven first on the scaled testbed and its digital twin, then on campus car parks.
